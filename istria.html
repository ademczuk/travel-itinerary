Before you leave

Three of these have a deadline. The fourth takes a tape measure.

Slovenian e-vinjeta buy by card

EUR 16 for a week, category 2A, which is what the Enyaq falls into. No EV discount. Pay by card, not bank transfer. A card payment is valid instantly. A bank transfer cannot start until four working days after the quote is created, and you do not have four working days.

Install the ELEN app before departure

Croatia's HEP ELEN chargers need their own app to start a session. Their own site describes roaming as still in progress, so an existing card may simply not work, and it holds EUR 53.09 on a DC session. Ionity is the main plan, so this is the backup, but it is not a backup you can install standing at a charger with a flat battery.

elen.hep.hr
Measure both boys against 150 cm Croatian law

Below 150 cm Croatia requires an approved child seat or booster, correctly fitted. There is an exception between 135 and 150 cm allowing a normal adult belt in the REAR only, never in front, but it only helps if they actually clear 135. Fine is EUR 130.

Your plan

Thursday 27 August

Vienna to Rovinj. 565 km, 6.3 hours of pure driving, so budget nine to ten hours door to door.

The drive, in order

Forecast that day: 29.6 C, 0 percent rain. The driest day of the whole window, which is a nice accident.

  1. 08:00, leave Vienna. A2 south toward Graz, then Spielfeld, Maribor, Ljubljana. This is the default road, not a clever detour: forcing the route through Maribor gives 564.9 km against 564.7 direct. Going via Villach and the Karawanken tunnel is 639.9 km, 75 km worse, and the Karawanken is a named late-August chokepoint with waits over an hour. You avoid it by doing nothing.
  2. About 200 km, charge at Ionity Kaiserwald, Raststation Kaiserwald, Premstaetten just south of Graz. Six stalls at 350 kW. Roughly 28 minutes.
  3. 434 km, Postojna Cave. Five kilometres off the route. See below.
  4. Charge at Petrol Kozina, 350 kW, 36 km and 24 minutes past the cave, and on the way rather than off it. not at the cave There is no fast charging at Postojna. Its own parking page offers eight AC spaces at 1.4 to 11 kW, hotel and VIP guests only. That is hours, not minutes. Arrive at the cave with enough charge to drive straight past it.
  5. Border at Kastel-Dragonja on the D510, joining the A9 at Umag. Schengen since 2023, so no passport or customs check, open around the clock.
  6. Rovinj by 18:30 to 19:30. Dinner somewhere that does not need a booking.

Friday 28 August

The hottest and wettest day of the window, so the anchor is the one that does not care about either.

Baredine Cave, then Porec

Forecast: 30.0 C, 38 percent rain. Highest chance of the trip. plan for heat plan for rain

Baredine is 30 minutes from Rovinj, open daily 10:00 to 18:00 with no rest day, tours every half hour, and no booking needed. It sits at a constant 14 C underground, which means it is exactly as good a day out whether it is pouring or 35 degrees. That is why it leads on this particular Friday rather than a beach.

Afterwards, lunch in Porec. If you want 40 minutes of UNESCO mosaics, the Euphrasian Basilica is there, adult EUR 10 and school age EUR 5, but note it is closed on Sundays, so it is today or not at all. Then back for the pool and the sea in the late afternoon.

Saturday 29 August

Pula, early. Or Brijuni, if the forecast has firmed up by then.

The Roman day

Forecast: 29.1 C, 36 percent rain.

Arena at 08:00, before the heat and before the crowds, and you are out in an hour. Then Aquarium Pula in Fort Verudela, which is the built-in indoor pivot for the same trip if the sky turns. Then a beach in the same corner of Verudela. Everything on this day is within a few minutes of everything else, which is the point of it.

Swap this with Brijuni if the forecast firms dry. Brijuni is the most memorable thing on offer and the only one an open sea crossing can cancel outright, so do not lock it in from Vienna. Boat from Fazana, 31 minutes from Rovinj, departures at 09:00, 10:00, 11:30, 13:30, 14:30 and 15:30. Around EUR 40 an adult and EUR 15 for a 7 to 18 year old, and free under 7, so the five year old goes free. Book at shop.np-brijuni.hr/en, which is the English shop; the bare domain sends you to the Croatian one. Do not schedule anything else that day: the crossing plus the island tour is the day.

One live caveat on the Arena. Judas Priest play there on 25 August and its own site already shows cut hours on the 23rd for the stage build. Nothing is flagged for your dates, but sound checks can land inside opening hours during any show week, so glance at the visitor page the evening before.

Two calls worth making

  • Glavani Park, the rope and adventure park near Barban, +385 98 224 314. Their own routes page lists a yellow, a blue and a black course and states no minimum age or height anywhere, so the "from age 2" marketing line is unconfirmed. Ask whether a five year old clears the yellow route before you build a morning around it.
  • Aquarium Porec, +385 52 428 720. A search result claimed it was temporarily closed. That looks like an artifact rather than a fact: the operator's own site shows normal July and August hours, and the third-party page the claim came from actually reads open. One call settles it.
  • Istra Premium Camping Resort, Funtana, +385 52 465 000. Not on Booking, so I could not check availability, but their own site lists a Bella Vista Villa for 4 plus 2 with a private pool, on a resort with a five-pool water park. If Porton goes, this is the call to make.

What to skip, and why

  • Cape Kamenjak. Beautiful, and the good coves need a long shadeless walk or a gravel track. Brutal at midday with a five year old.
  • Motovun on foot. Steep cobbled climb in full August sun with no shade until the top.
  • Lim Fjord and Pazin Abyss zipline courses. Height and age minimums rule out the five year old, and the standalone fjord cruise is 45 minutes of looking at a wall.
  • Anything stacked on the Brijuni day. The crossing plus the island tour is the day.

What I got wrong while planning this

Three corrections, because a page that only shows the polished answer teaches you nothing about how much to trust it.

One. I told you Postojna Cave was the charging stop, because it sits at 434 km of a 569 km drive and that is exactly where the second charge falls. It was a lovely coincidence and it was wrong. I inferred a charger from a convenient distance rather than checking, and the cave's own page says AC only, hotel guests only. Kozina, 24 minutes further, is the real stop.

Two. I flagged Aquarium Porec as temporarily closed on the strength of a search summary. The operator's own site says otherwise and so does the page the claim came from. A summary of a page is not the page.

Three, and the biggest. I told you Rovinj was full. It is not. Its hotels are full for a party of four, which is a much narrower claim, and I had measured one operator and generalised it to a whole town. Twenty-five apartments and villas came back available the moment I asked the question properly.
