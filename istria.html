Lone Bay, the beach you asked for (done, tap to open)

Lone Bay, the beach you asked for

Ul. Luje Adamovica 31 is Hotel Lone. The beach is Plaza Lone, inside the Zlatni Rt forest park, 1.5 km south of the center and about 2.2 km from your campsite.

What it actually is. Istra.hr describes it as "uredena i posuta sljunkom", laid out and covered with pebbles, under "guste krosnje stogodisnjih stabala", the dense canopy of century-old trees. Changing cabins, showers, WC, loungers and umbrellas, restaurants on site. It looks back at the old town and across to Katarina island, which is why it is the picture everyone takes. The Rovinj tourist board's own line is that it is the most visited beach in Rovinj by residents and guests alike. Read that as both a recommendation and a warning: in the last week of August it is busy.
How long is it? Nobody official says, and the number you will find is not the beach. You asked for a large pebble beach, so this is the one figure that matters and I could not get it. Neither istra.hr nor the Rovinj tourist board publishes a length for Plaza Lone. The 200 m that circulates is the SETNICA, the promenade: istra.hr says the rebuilt walkway from the ACI marina to the Zlatni Rt entrance runs "oko dvjestotinjak metara", about two hundred meters. That is the path, not the shoreline, and quoting it as the beach length would make this beach sound a third the size of Vestar on a number that measures a footpath. One commercial site says 300 m; I am not passing that on as a fact because no official source corroborates it. If length is the deciding factor, Vestar is the answer, at a verified 700 m. Lone wins on shade, facilities and the view, not on size that anyone will confirm.
One caution the brochures and I disagree about, so here is both. Croatian beach listings describe Lone as suited to all swimmers and especially families with children. A different description of the same beach says the deepening begins right from the shoreline. Those are not the same beach for a five year old. There is no shallow sandy shelf to stand around in the way Vestar has. Take the armbands you would take anywhere, keep him inside arm's reach for the first ten minutes, and you will know within one swim which description is true. Water shoes either way, it is pebble.

Emergency 112, everywhere

112 is the emergency number in Slovenia and in Croatia and across the whole EU. What I got wrong while planning this (done, tap to open)

What I got wrong while planning this

Three corrections, because a page that only shows the polished answer teaches you nothing about how much to trust it.

One. I told you Postojna Cave was the charging stop, because it sits at 434 km of a 569 km drive and that is exactly where the second charge falls. It was a lovely coincidence and it was wrong. I inferred a charger from a convenient distance rather than checking, and the cave's own page says AC only, hotel guests only. Kozina, 24 minutes further, is the real stop.

Two. I flagged Aquarium Porec as temporarily closed on the strength of a search summary. The operator's own site says otherwise and so does the page the claim came from. A summary of a page is not the page.

Three, and the biggest. I told you Rovinj was full. It is not. Its hotels are full for a party of four, which is a much narrower claim, and I had measured one operator and generalized it to a whole town. Twenty-five apartments and villas came back available the moment I asked the question properly.
Three more from the 25 August pass, and the first is the one that would have cost you a day.

Four. I had Porton Nature Hideouts in the stay table and separately described a base "at Polari, near Porton Nature Hideouts". Those are opposite ends of town. Porton Biondi is 700 m north of the old town; Polari is a campsite several kilometers south. Because I had them conflated, the whole eating section was built around driving to dinner, and the two konobas I called "nearest the base" are the two furthest from it. The renaming from Camping Porton Biondi to Porton Nature Hideouts is what let one place look like two.

Five. The drive said charge at 200 km and again at 470 km and left it there. That is a 270 km middle leg needing about 58 kWh from a 79 kWh battery, so leaving Kaiserwald at 80 percent arrives at Kozina on empty. I had checked that both chargers existed and never checked the car could reach the second one. Existence is not reachability.

Six, and it is a process failure rather than a fact. While writing the Grand Park row I typed a phone number without looking it up. I caught it, deleted it, then searched: it was correct. That is luck, not method, and a number that happens to be right is indistinguishable on the page from one that is invented. The two numbers in that row now come from Maistra's own listing. If you find anything else on this page that looks like a plausible number with no source behind it, distrust it.
